About Genesis

Where Learning Meets Possibility

Genesis Knowledge Centre, established under the guidance of Manba-Ul-Uloom in 2025, is a non-profit educational initiative dedicated to nurturing young minds and preparing future generations for an ever-changing world.

✓STEM, ICT, Robotics and English development
✓Mentorship, leadership and career guidance
✓Practical and project-based learning
